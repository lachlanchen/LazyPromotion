The tools started with different problems. L & N came from confusing two sounds. Bunko keeps reading help beside a page. EchoMind brings language help into a conversation. They belong together, but finding them should not mean searching through a long list of repositories.

[LazyingArt Platform](https://platform.lazying.art/) is their front door. You can browse the apps, read how they work, watch a short demonstration and follow the official store link. There is no account wall around the catalogue.

The first part of the shared account is now live: [Platform sign-in](https://platform.lazying.art/account) opens the central LazyingArt account service and brings you back to your dashboard. You can register without an invitation and sign in with a password. Google, Apple and GitHub sign-in are still being connected; they are not available in this shared flow yet.

## One identity, separate places to work

The point is to stop making people start over every time they try another tool. Platform is the first working connection, not a claim that every app has already switched. Other apps keep their current entry points until their own integration is ready.

That should not turn every app into one shared folder. A memo belongs in your memo workspace; a paper and its private notes belong in your reading workspace. Signing in identifies you. It does not publish your notes, buy an app or connect a wallet.

EchoMind access also stays separate from a general LazyingArt account. Creating the account does not grant paid features, app-store purchases or access to a service with its own membership rules.

## The small technical detail that matters

The password goes to the central account service, not to every app. Platform receives a limited sign-in result. Existing private workspaces cannot be merged just because two accounts happen to use the same email address.

Coin access is a separate choice on the dashboard. If you allow the summary read, an unlinked account is shown as needing a link—not as having a zero balance. Account linking is not available yet. You can withdraw that permission without signing out of Platform; signing in does not connect a wallet or distribute coins.

## What you can use today

Browse [the platform](https://platform.lazying.art/), choose a tool and follow its official download or web link. The account is optional for browsing: you do not need to register just to find L & N, Bunko or EchoMind. Use the Account page if you want to try the shared password sign-in.

The aim is simple: spend less time finding the right app and getting back into it, and more time doing the thing you came for.
