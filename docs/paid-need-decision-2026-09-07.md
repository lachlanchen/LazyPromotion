# Paid-need decision — 2026-09-07

The first-USD-1,000 plan should stay focused on paid buyer work. Current grants,
prizes, and bounties either pay too slowly, depend on a low-probability award,
or require eligibility evidence that is not yet present. They are useful future
funding routes, not substitutes for customer revenue.
