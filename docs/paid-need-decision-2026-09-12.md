## Decision

1. Keep technical verification and infrastructure work ahead of social volume.
2. Complete an application only when every personal claim is supported and the
   data, privacy, assessment, and identity gates can be handled truthfully.
3. Prefer a small paid milestone over an open-ended promise.
4. Keep L & N as the lower-friction product-led path and OpenHI as the strongest
   high-ticket public proof.
5. Count nothing until selection, accepted work, delivery, and payment are
   evidenced separately.

fields. No Connects were purchased and no proposal was submitted. The current
Postiz queue is already broad enough; the next social action should answer a
real question or present new checkable proof.
